Auditing Odoo Helpdesk Closures: Stages, Auto-Close and Ratings

Review how tickets close in Odoo 19 Helpdesk: folded stages, automatic closing, customer closure and ratings, and the reports that show problems.

Short answer: In Odoo 19 Helpdesk a ticket is closed when it sits in a stage that is Folded in Kanban. That can happen three ways: an agent moves it, Automatic Closing moves it after a period of inactivity, or the customer clicks Close Ticket. A closure review checks each route for tickets that were closed without being solved, and uses the Tickets Analysis and Customer Ratings reports to see the result. Helpdesk is an Enterprise app.

Applies to

  • Odoo version: Odoo 19. I compared the closing, ratings and reporting pages with the 20.0 versions. The text is the same.
  • Edition: Enterprise only. Field names and behaviour come from the documentation.
  • Apps: Helpdesk. The rating machinery is a standard mixin in the public Odoo source.

How can a ticket end up closed?

First, manually. The ticket moves to a folded stage, set through the stage’s gear icon and Edit, with Folded in Kanban ticked. The gear’s own Fold option is a display toggle and closes nothing.

Second, automatically, using Automatic Closing under Self-Service on the team. You choose a Move to Stage, After days of inactivity (7 by default) and, optionally, the stages to watch under In Stages.

Third, by the customer: Closure by Customers adds a Close Ticket button to the portal view of the ticket.

Each route leaves the same trace, a ticket in a folded stage, which is why a review has to ask how it got there.

Where does automatic closing send tickets?

This is the first thing I would check, because the default is easy to get wrong. If the team has exactly one folded stage, that is the default target. If it has several, the default is the one that appears first in the pipeline. If none is folded, the default is the last stage.

So a team with a folded Cancelled stage placed before a folded Solved stage will see inactive tickets land in Cancelled, and reports will say the customer cancelled. Open the Move to Stage field and pick the stage that matches what you want to say.

Two more details from the documentation. The inactivity count ignores the working calendar. And without In Stages, the rule is not restricted to the stages where silence is expected. The docs’ own example restricts it to a Customer Feedback stage, because tickets stuck in New or In Progress point to a workload problem and closing them would hide it.

What do ratings say about closures?

The rating request is an email template, Helpdesk: Ticket Rating Request, set on a stage through Set an Email Template on Stages once Customer Ratings is ticked on the team. The documentation says the template belongs on a folded stage, since a folded stage means the ticket is closed.

That has a consequence for the case above. The template is sent when a ticket moves into the stage that holds it, so if automatic closing targets that stage, customers are asked to rate a ticket that closed for inactivity. Decide whether that is what you want.

Ratings reach the ticket chatter, with the comment. Internally they map to Rating (1-5) in the reports: Dissatisfied is 1, Okay is 3 and Satisfied is 5. An average of 3 could be everyone choosing Okay or an even split of happy and unhappy customers, so read the comments before drawing conclusions. In the 19.0 source the rating mixin exposes fields such as rating_avg and rating_last_value, which is useful if you build your own dashboard.

On a rating’s detail page, Visible Internally Only hides it from public and portal users.

What should a monthly closure review look at?

Open Helpdesk ‣ Reporting ‣ Tickets Analysis, filter Closed, and group by stage and team. Check the count that landed in each folded stage. Add Working Hours to Close and compare it with Hours Open, the latter being calendar time. Then open Helpdesk ‣ Reporting ‣ Customer Ratings and read the comments on the lowest ratings.

Finally, read a handful of tickets that auto-closed. Did the customer have the last word or the agent? The report cannot tell you that, but a few minutes in the chatter can.

One metric quirk: the Average Hours to Respond measure ignores messages sent while the ticket was in a folded stage. After you change which stages are folded, comparisons with older months are not like for like.

When it fits and when it doesn’t

Automatic closing fits support where waiting for a customer reply is normal and a stale ticket is clutter. It does not fit a queue where unanswered tickets usually mean the team is behind. Customer closure fits when customers use the portal; it fits poorly for customers who only ever reply by email.

A worked example

A company that sells subscription boxes has stages New, In Progress, Waiting for Customer, Solved and Cancelled, with Solved and Cancelled both folded. The first review shows many auto-closed tickets in Cancelled and a low average rating on a few of them. The cause: Move to Stage was left on the default, which is the first folded stage in the pipeline.

The fix is to set Move to Stage to Solved, limit In Stages to Waiting for Customer, and keep the rating template on Solved only.

Common mistakes

  • Using Fold instead of Folded in Kanban. Nothing is closed.
  • Putting the rating template on every closing stage. Customers get asked after cancellations.
  • Never opening the Customer Ratings report. The comments are the useful part.
  • Changing folded stages mid-quarter. Trends stop being comparable.

Frequently asked questions

Where does Odoo Helpdesk move tickets when they are closed automatically?

To the stage in the Move to Stage field. If the team has several folded stages, the default is the one that comes first in the pipeline, so check it is not a cancelled or rejected stage.

Can customers close their own Helpdesk tickets?

Yes. Tick Closure by Customers under Self-Service on the team, and customers see a Close Ticket button when they view the ticket in the portal.

Which stage should carry the customer rating email?

A folded stage that means the issue was resolved. Customers should only be asked to rate once their ticket is closed, and the template is sent when a ticket moves into the stage that holds it.
