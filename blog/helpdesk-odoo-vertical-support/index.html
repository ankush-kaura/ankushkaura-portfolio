Odoo Helpdesk for Clinics and Vet Practices: Admin Requests

How Odoo 19 Helpdesk can route the administrative requests of clinics and vet practices, and where privacy and clinical records need care.

Published on 30 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

  • Odoo
  • Odoo 19
  • Helpdesk
  • Customer Service
  • Healthcare
  • Veterinary
  • Support
  • SME

Short answer: Odoo Helpdesk suits the paperwork around a clinic or veterinary practice: appointment changes, invoice questions, document requests, product orders and complaints. It does not suit the clinical side. Give each kind of request its own team, keep the visibility tight, collect as little personal detail as the form needs, and use a different, approved system for clinical records. Helpdesk is an Enterprise app, so this assumes you have Enterprise.

Applies to

  • Odoo version: Odoo 19. I read the same Helpdesk documentation pages in 20.0. Only the Field Service part of after-sales is worded differently there (planning through the Planning app).
  • Edition: Enterprise only.
  • Apps: Helpdesk, with optional Sales, Accounting, Inventory, Repairs, Knowledge and Website. Nothing here is legal or clinical advice. Rules differ by country and profession, so ask a local adviser.

One inbox mixes requests that need different people

A reception address collects everything: “Can I move my appointment?”, “Why was I billed twice?”, “Please send my records to another practice”, “My dog’s food has not arrived”. In a shared mailbox these look the same.

In Helpdesk, each kind of request gets a team under Helpdesk ‣ Configuration ‣ Helpdesk Teams, with its own email alias, members and stages. The billing team can then use a different pipeline from the reception team. Automatic assignment can dispatch by tag, and the docs note that untagged tickets stay unassigned, so agree the tag list first.

What usually needs customising is anything involving the appointment itself. Helpdesk tracks a request about an appointment; it does not manage the diary. If tickets need to update another system, plan that integration as a separate piece of work.

Sensitive wording lands in tickets whether you asked for it or not

This is the part I would think hardest about. Whatever a person writes in an email to a team alias becomes the ticket: the subject is the title and the body goes into Description and the chatter. Website forms are the same, except that you decide which fields exist.

So design the form to ask for only what the request needs. The sidebar editor on the form lets you add a Description line under a field, set a Placeholder and mark fields as Required. Use that space to say what not to send. Keep the team description free of internal wording, because the documentation says it is published on the public form.

Visibility is the second control. A private team shows tickets only to invited internal users, and followers can be added per ticket. Also watch external followers on the team: they are notified of updates even if they cannot open the ticket in the portal.

Retention, access rights and who may export data are outside what the Helpdesk pages cover. Settle them with your adviser before go-live.

An urgent message is not a ticket

A support queue is a poor emergency channel, and nobody should rely on a ticket being read promptly. Priority stars exist, and SLA policies can match on them, but that tracks office response times, not clinical urgency.

Put the instruction where people will see it: in the form text and in the acknowledgment email people receive when a ticket is created (the Request Acknowledgment template sits on the first stage by default). Wording such as “for urgent matters, call the practice” belongs there. Then agents know to move urgent cases out of the queue to the approved route rather than answer them in the chatter.

Product orders, refunds and returns leave the ticket

Practices that sell food, supplements or accessories get purchase problems as well. With the after-sales options on the team, a ticket can issue a credit note, generate a coupon, start a return or create a repair order. Each needs the right data: refunds need a posted invoice, and the Return button only appears if the customer has a recorded delivery.

Enabling these may install other modules, so decide the apps first. A practice that only handles administration may want none of them.

Repeated questions belong in articles

Opening hours, how to request records, how to prepare for a visit: answer once. The Help Center uses Knowledge articles, and a clipboard box inside an article can be sent as a message or added to the description from a ticket. Articles must be published one by one to appear on the website.

I would avoid the Community Forum option for health topics. The docs describe converting tickets into forum posts after editing out proprietary information, which is one more place for a slip.

Feedback needs a privacy check too

Ratings are collected by an email template on a folded stage, and the comments land in the ticket chatter. On a rating’s detail page, Visible Internally Only hides it from public and portal users. For sensitive services I would tick that by default and review the comments before quoting any of them.

Where to start

Start with one team, private visibility, one alias and a form with very few fields. Add a second team when a real queue needs it. Test with invented data: an email, a form, a reassignment, a closed ticket and a refund.

After a few weeks, open Helpdesk ‣ Reporting ‣ Tickets Analysis and look at Hours to First Response and Working Hours to Close by team. Those two measures tell a practice manager whether reception or billing is the bottleneck, which is a better basis for staffing than a feeling.

Then read what Odoo Helpdesk does, the setup guide and the common mistakes before widening access. There is a longer screen by screen walkthrough of a similar scenario on sajjalbajaj.in.

Frequently asked questions

Should clinical details go into an Odoo Helpdesk ticket?

I would keep them out. Helpdesk is built for administrative requests, and anything a customer writes by email or form ends up in the ticket and chatter. Keep clinical records in the system your practice has approved for them and check the rules with a local data protection adviser.

Can a Helpdesk team be hidden from other staff?

Yes. A team's visibility can be set to invited internal users only, so staff see the tickets they follow. The documentation notes that this is configured per team and can be changed later.

Can a Helpdesk ticket lead to a refund or return?

Yes, when the matching after-sales option is enabled on the team and the related apps are installed. Refunds need a posted invoice, and the Return button needs a recorded delivery for the customer.
