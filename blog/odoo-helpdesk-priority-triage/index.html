Triage Odoo Helpdesk Tickets: Priority, Tags and Assignment

How priority stars, tags and assignment work together in Odoo 19 Helpdesk triage, and how a priority change can alter an SLA deadline.

Short answer: Triage in Odoo 19 Helpdesk is three fields doing separate jobs. Priority (zero to three stars) controls ordering and can decide which SLA policy applies. Tags say what the ticket is about and can decide who receives it. Assignment hands it to a person. Agree what each star means before launch, because a changed star can change an SLA deadline. Helpdesk is an Enterprise app.

Applies to

  • Odoo version: Odoo 19. I compared the receiving-tickets, SLA, reporting and main Helpdesk pages with the 20.0 versions. The priority, tag and assignment text matches.
  • Edition: Enterprise only. Everything here about Helpdesk fields comes from the documentation.
  • Apps: Helpdesk. Time Off affects who can be assigned, and Timesheets adds a Services criterion to SLAs.

What does priority control?

Every ticket has a Priority field shown as stars. The meaning is fixed: no stars is Low, one is Medium, two is High, three is Urgent. A new ticket is Low, and higher priority tickets sort to the top of the Kanban and list views.

Two consequences follow from that. Because the default is Low, a ticket nobody has looked at is, in the data, the least urgent one. And because the stars can be set on the Kanban card as well as on the ticket, changing them takes one click.

I did not find a documented way to let customers set priority on the website form, so I would treat it as an agent decision.

How do tags and assignment fit in?

On the team form, Automatic Assignment offers three modes: equal total tickets, equal open tickets, and Dispatch tickets based on tags. In the tag mode you click Configure tags handled by team members and link each ticket tag, such as Pricing or IT, to one or more members. Tickets with no tag stay unassigned, and people with scheduled time off are skipped.

That makes the tag list part of your routing design rather than a decoration. A long, loosely named tag list means tickets quietly miss the dispatcher. I would keep tags to the real skill areas of the team and add an “Other” tag that goes to whoever triages.

How does priority connect to SLAs?

An SLA policy under Helpdesk ‣ Configuration ‣ SLA Policies matches on team, priority, tags, customers and, with Timesheets, services. The detail that surprises people is how the priority criterion behaves. The policy only applies once the ticket’s stars match, and a policy with nothing selected applies only to Low tickets.

The documentation also warns that changing a ticket’s priority can change its SLA deadline. If several policies match, only the earliest deadline is shown, then the next one after it passes. So an agent who raises a ticket from two stars to three is not just flagging it, they are choosing a different deadline.

Can triage be automated?

Partly, and I would be careful here. Dispatch by tags and SLA matching are configuration. Setting priority automatically from the content of a message is not something I found in the Helpdesk documentation. Odoo’s automation rules can act on records when a trigger fires, but I have not checked how they behave on Helpdesk fields, and that would be a customisation to design and test, not a setting to tick.

If you go that way, start with a rule that only suggests or flags, and keep a human in the loop for priority.

How do I check that triage works?

Use Helpdesk ‣ Reporting ‣ Tickets Analysis. The documentation’s use case: choose the measure Working Hours to Close, group by Priority and filter on Closed. Compare how long Urgent tickets take against Low ones. If they take about the same, priority is not driving behaviour. Add Hours to First Response to see whether the early reaction differs, and use the SLA Status Analysis report to see failures over time.

When it fits and when it doesn’t

A four-level scale fits teams with different promise levels for different problems. It does not fit a team of two who answer everything the same day: there, extra stars are noise. Keep the scheme as small as the real difference in response.

A worked example

A company hiring out event equipment has one team, with tags Audio, Lighting and Delivery. The team writes down what each star means in one sentence, for instance Urgent means an event is happening within the day and nothing can replace the item.

A customer emails that a speaker has failed. The ticket arrives at Low with the Audio tag and goes to the audio specialist, who raises it to Urgent because of the event. That change attaches the Urgent SLA and its earlier deadline, and the ticket jumps to the top of the board. The reverse also works: a question about next month’s invoice stays Low and waits.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving every ticket at Low. The SLAs never engage.
  • Assuming an empty priority on an SLA means every ticket. It means Low only.
  • Skipping tags and choosing tag dispatch. Nothing gets assigned.
  • Letting stars drift. Review the definition quarterly with the team.

Frequently asked questions

What do the stars mean on an Odoo Helpdesk ticket?

Zero stars is Low, one is Medium, two is High and three is Urgent. New tickets start at Low, and higher priorities appear first in the Kanban and list views.

Does an SLA policy with no priority selected apply to all tickets?

No. According to the Odoo 19 documentation, a policy with no priority selected applies only to Low priority tickets, meaning those with zero stars.

What happens to a ticket with no tag when assignment is by tags?

It stays unassigned. Dispatch by tags only assigns tickets that carry a tag handled by a team member.
